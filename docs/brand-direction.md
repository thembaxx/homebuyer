# Name and positioning

The recommended creative direction is **Housight** (house + insight), paired with
the descriptive product line **Homebuyer’s Reality Check**. Keep the current
dimensional house/check icon, warm neutrals and sage/lime palette.

The live working name remains **HomeBuyer Reality Check** until a name is chosen.
`src/lib/brand.ts` is the single source for the wordmark, metadata, documentation,
manifest and PDF branding. Browser storage keys and the repository name remain
stable so a future rename does not discard saved homes.

| Direction               | Name                    | Why it fits                                                                | Tradeoff                                                         |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Recommended             | Housight                | Connects a home with insight; concise enough for an app icon and wordmark. | Explain pronunciation and retain the descriptive product line.   |
| Descriptive             | HomeBuyer Reality Check | Says what the product does and preserves the existing identity.            | Generic words offer less distinctive brand search.               |
| South African influence | KhayaCheck              | A locally influenced direction with a clear checking cue.                  | Confirm the language treatment and audience fit before choosing. |

Positioning: **Know the cost of living in the home before you commit to buying it.**
Tagline: **Evidence before emotion.**
Landing message: **Fall for the home. Know the reality.**

Brand voice is warm, clear and specific. Say “your assumptions,” “dated public
evidence” and “questions to investigate.” Avoid “street safety score,” “verified
valuation” or guarantees the product cannot support.

## Preliminary name screening

The web review on 12 October 2026 found direct conflicts for several early ideas:

- [BeforeKeys](https://beforekeysapp.com/) already operates a rental-documentation product.
- [OWNWARD](https://ownward.co/) already operates a digital product/content brand;
  [Ownward](https://www.ownward.app/) also operates an income-protection product.
- [KEYLENS](https://www.prophet.com/) is already associated with a consulting business,
  and the name appears in other software products.
- [HuisLens](https://www.oozo.nl/bedrijven/tilburg/heikant/heikant-zuid-oost) appears as a Dutch software business.

Housight appeared in an older [Japanese property-service naming proposal](https://crowdworks.jp/public/jobs/339523/proposal_products/users/269291).
That result is neither an active South African product nor evidence that the name
is available. Housight and KhayaCheck are creative shortlist candidates, not
confirmed trademark or domain availability. No domain has been registered or
assumed by this implementation.

## Search positioning

Use the distinctive brand with descriptive page titles, rather than forcing
every keyword into the name:

- South African Property Due Diligence
- The Monthly Cost of Owning a Home in South Africa
- Data Sources & Report Methodology

The personal report workspace is marked `noindex`; public pages supply the
search and AI discovery content. Future R99–R299 pricing is clearly separated
from the currently free MVP.
